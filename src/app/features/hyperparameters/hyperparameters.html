  • Theta / Carryover — "how long an ad's impact lingers": When you spend on a channel this week, does its effect disappear right away, or does some of it carry over into next week and the week after? Theta is a number from 0 to 1. Close to 0 means the effect is basically gone by next week (typical of a direct-response channel like paid search — someone searches, clicks, converts, done). Close to 1 means the effect fades very slowly, week after week (more typical of brand/TV spend, which people keep remembering after the ad stops running). This is a real number sent to the model.
  • Alpha — "how much spend it takes before a channel starts to saturate": Every channel eventually hits a point of diminishing returns — the 10th dollar of daily spend rarely does as much as the 1st. Alpha (0.05 to 1 in this preview) controls roughly where that point sits along the spend axis. A higher alpha pushes that point further out, so the channel looks "un-saturated" over a wider spend range. Alpha only shapes the chart on this page — there's no real "alpha" field saved to the model. Only Gamma below is actually sent and used for training.
  • Gamma — "how sharply returns flatten out after that point": Once a channel starts saturating, how abrupt is the flattening? A low gamma flattens quickly and sharply right after the half-saturation point; a high gamma bends more gradually, so the channel keeps giving a little more for a while longer before it truly flattens. This is the real "saturation" value that gets saved. Gamma must be greater than 0 (never exactly 0) - the slider here goes from just above 0 to 3 (a practical range for realistic curves), though the model itself has no hard upper limit, so a value above 3 is technically valid, it's just not reachable from this slider.
  • Automatic Optimization: Applies a real suggested value computed from this channel's actual spend history, your saved training date range, and any real channel combinations - the same real weeks and channels that will actually train, not a guess. It's disabled for a channel that doesn't have at least 4 real weeks of spend to compute a suggestion from. It is still not Meridian's own trained answer - that only comes from a real training run - so values it sets are still marked "Estimated."
  • Channels are taken from what you saved in Configure. Valid ranges: Theta (carryover) 0–1, Gamma (saturation) greater than 0. You don't have to set both values for every channel, or touch every channel at all - only what you actually apply here gets saved; anything left untouched uses the modeling engine's own real defaults.
